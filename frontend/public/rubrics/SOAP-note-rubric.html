Pharmacotherapy Skills Lab — SOAP / MTM note rubric

Educational rubric for UTEP EMR practice. Not for real clinical grading outside the course.

CriterionMeets expectationsNeeds revision
Reason for encounterClear chief complaint and contextVague or missing
Medication experienceAdherence, access, and tolerability addressedOmits adherence or barriers
Objective dataRelevant labs/vitals cited with valuesUnsupported assertions
Drug therapy problemSpecific, prioritized DTPsGeneric or incorrect DTPs
RecommendationsActionable, evidence-aligned planUnsafe or incomplete plan
Follow-upMonitoring and timeframe statedNo follow-up plan
